# BiogasFlex v0.9.18 — 15-minute multiscale CHP optimizer verification

**Scientific scope:** reproducible mathematical verification of **fictional**
heat-constrained, buffered CHP LP and finite-horizon on/off MILP.
A solution that satisfies synthetic constraints does not establish
the real flexibility, safety, physical efficiency or dispatch economics
of an identified biogas-fired reciprocating engine.

## Hypothesis and methodological distinction

Can the inherited, unmodified v0.9.5 ramp-constrained LP and v0.9.7
elapsed-hour binary CHP MILP maintain gas and heat conservation,
commitment/ramp rules and a numerically consistent economic objective
over **96**, **192** and **672** quarter-hour intervals?

These are 24-hour, 48-hour and 168-hour cases, respectively.
The full weekly run is a **research solver-scaling test, not seven
consecutive actual plant operating days**.

Each scenario has exactly **15-minute UTC intervals** beginning
2020-01-01T00:00:00Z; all timestamps, arrivals, hypothetical gas/heat
parameters and price patterns are generated by deterministic source
code. **None** is a recorded 2020 SE3 price, a preauction forecast,
an actual electricity award, or a measured engine output.
The use of the label SE3 specifies the solver price-zone input only,
and **does not imply that the Japanese measured Kitakyushu plant is in
Sweden or trades in SE3**. v0.9.17 real observations remain separate.

### F1 — forced zero-buffer commitment MILP

Original implementation: `experimental_v097.optimize_commitment_milp`.

- In each synthetic day, gas production is **0 MW_LHV for the first
  12h**, followed by **2 MW_LHV for the next 12h**.
- Gas buffer capacity **0 MWh_LHV**; zero initial and terminal stock;
  no flaring/loss. In each time slot the exact gas equality gives
  `u_i = production_i`.
- Constant invented electrical conversion `η_e = 0.4`, nameplate
  `0.8 MW_e`, minimum-on `0.2 MW_e`.
- Constant invented thermal conversion `η_th = 0.2`,
  instantaneous heat service `0.15 MW_th), backup heat maximum
  `0.2 MW_th`, heat dump maximum `0.3 MW_th`. No thermal storage.
- Binary initially **OFF**, 0.5h minimum ON and OFF, 0.4 MW_e/h
  within-ON midpoint ramp. Transition steps are exempt from ramp,
  as in the inherited v0.9.7 surrogate.
- Startup and shutdown costs `€5` and `€2`, respectively;
  CHP variable cost €1/MWh_e, backup heat €3/MWh_th,
  rejected heat €1/MWh_th. All are fictional.
- Hypothetical 4-hour price blocks each day:
  `[20, 95, 35, 110, 25, 85]` EUR/MWh_e.

**Independent algebraic optimum certificate.** At every quarter hour:

```text
  s_(i+1) = s_i + (gas_production_i - gas_use_i)*0.25h
  s_i = s_(i+1) = 0  -> gas_use_i = gas_production_i
  P_e_i = eta_e * gas_use_i = 0 or 0.8 MW_e
  z_i = 1 only at a (0 -> 1) production boundary
  v_i = 1 only at a (1 -> 0) production boundary
```

The gas equality and power minimum/nameplate force **all gas flows,
electrical outputs and binary transitions**. The heat-allocation
variables can have other *feasible* splits, but both backup-heat
and dumped-heat penalties are strictly positive. Therefore a
**cost-minimal optimum** must maximize contemporaneous useful CHP
heat `q_useful=min(η_th·u, demand)`, which fixes auxiliary and
dumped heat and establishes the exact independently derived
optimal objective for this particular reduced zero-buffer model.
This is **not** a theorem for arbitrary buffered CHP scheduling.

For each daily block, the independent witness predicts:
`24 MWh_LHV` gas consumed; `9.6 MWh_e` generated; `704 EUR`
gross fictional electricity value; `5.4 EUR` backup-heat cost,
`3 EUR` heat rejection cost and `9.6 EUR` variable CHP cost.
A first startup costs `5 EUR`; a subsequent interday shutdown
costs `2 EUR`. Exact net objectives, provided all modeled constraints
are feasible, are:
- 96 intervals: `€681` (one startup, no stop).
- 192 intervals: `€1,360` (two startups, one stop).
- 672 intervals: `€4,755` (seven startups, six stops).

These are **synthetic model contributions**, not booked profit,
metered generation sold at the grid boundary, or actual CHP OPEX.

Because the case is structurally determined, it exercises long-horizon
model construction, presolve, dwell constraints, switching accounting
and energy ledgers without testing difficult branch-and-bound trade-offs.
**A fast solve is not evidence that general 672-slot MILPs will be fast.**

### F2 — continuously ON gas-buffer LP

Original implementation:
`experimental_v095.optimize_operating_surrogate`.

- Constant 1 MW_LHV fictional gas inflow with source-zero flaring/loss.
- `η_e = 0.4`, `η_th = 0.2`, electric rating `0.8 MW_e`.
- Instantaneous heat service `0.2 MW_th`, auxiliary maximum
  `0.25 MW_th`, heat dump maximum `0.5 MW_th`.
- Always-ON lower output `0.1 MW_e`, starting at `0.4 MW_e`;
  up/down midpoint ramp `0.4 MW_e/h`.
- Auxiliary, dump and CHP variable costs `€4/MWh_th`,
  `€1/MWh_th` and `€0.5/MWh_e`.
- Deterministic alternating 4-hour fictional price blocks as above.
- **Paired scenarios** under identical hours/prices/heat/fuel:
  (a) **no gas store** `C=0, initial=0`; and (b)
  `C=0.75 MWh_LHV, initial=terminal=0.375 MWh_LHV`.

The no-buffer case fixes `u_i=1 MW_LHV` everywhere, providing an
independent algebraic baseline. The positive-buffer LP has genuine
storage-shifting freedom, coupled heat backup/dump choices and ramp
limits. Its objective is independently recomputed from the returned
quarter-hour physical ledger; an inherited unconstrained heat LP
provides an upper bound. The **new stdlib auditor does not possess
an independent LP dual certificate**. We therefore do not label
the long-horizon ramp-LP objective independently certified globally
optimal: the HiGHS solver reports optimization status, while we
independently verify its primal physics, accounting and bounds.

A storage-enabled feasible region contains its fixed-stock comparator;
a negative objective increment beyond tolerance is rejected.

### F3 — intentionally infeasible controls

Three distinct **96-slot** synthetic examples must be rejected:

1. The first forced-OFF quarter has zero engine heat and
   `0.15 MW_th` demand, but backup capacity is only
   `0.10 MW_th`. No storage/heat substitute exists.
2. Engine nameplate is reduced to `0.6 MW_e` while forced
   2 MW_LHV fuel use still requires `0.8 MW_e`.
3. A required ON dwell of `12.25 h` starts at hour 12 of
   a 24-hour horizon and cannot finish by the terminal boundary,
   per the already explicit v0.9.7 commitment-end policy.

These are solver/classified *mathematical* infeasibilities, not
observed CHP equipment failures or dropped real delivery bids.

## Independent witness, arithmetic and scientific gates

`scripts/audit_v0918_independent.py` uses **only Python stdlib**
on the complete generated JSON — it imports **no `biogasflex`
source, NumPy, SciPy or MILP solver**. Every 15-minute row is
independently checked for:

- UTC quarter-hour continuity, duration, source identity and original
  fictional price/production input;
- gas inventory dynamics and unchanged terminal inventory;
- electric power `P=η_e u`, heat production, useful heat and
  external heat conservation (no hidden heat storage);
- rating, lower output, gas-buffer, backup and dump caps;
- midpoint on-mode ramp inequalities and valid on/off transition
  binaries; for forced discrete cases, elapsed-hour dwell events;
- scalar objective and full ledger recomputation, nonnegative
  starts/stops, MIP reported zero gap, and separately named
  **case-scoped exact solution** for zero-buffer forced MILP;
- no mistaken real CHP, market forecasting or independently
  proven dual LP labels.

The full benchmark runs in a dedicated **Ubuntu Python 3.13**
job, generating both the complete solver schedule and a separate
independent verification JSON. The inherited cross-platform matrix
runs lightweight 4/8-slot adversarial regression tests and the
original full numerical research suite, **not** full week MILP on
all nine Python/OS combinations. No hard wall-clock performance
threshold is imposed: runner scheduling, model building, CPU and
SciPy/HiGHS versions materially affect elapsed time.

## Reproduce

Windows PowerShell (small regression, all prior experiments preserved):

```powershell
git pull origin main
python -m pip install -e .
.\scripts\reproduce_v0918.ps1
python -m biogasflex benchmark-v0918 --smoke
```

Full 96/192/672 optimization and independent verification on your
machine (all output **outside** the checked-out Git tree):

```powershell
$full = Join-Path $env:TEMP 'biogasflex_v0918_full.json'
$proof = Join-Path $env:TEMP 'biogasflex_v0918_independent.json'
python -m biogasflex benchmark-v0918 --output $full
python scripts/audit_v0918_independent.py --input $full --output $proof
```

`.github/workflows/v0918-week-benchmark.yml`.
