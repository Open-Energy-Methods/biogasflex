# v0.5 baseline assumptions

- Constant gas input 0.6 MW on an unspecified but consistently applied LHV energy basis, rather than a physical Nm3/h measurement.
- Illustrative 0.4 MW electrical rating, 0.4 electrical efficiency, 0.45 thermal efficiency: **not observed at a named plant**.
- Electricity delivered is 0.24 MW. Nameplate headroom ratio 0.4/0.24 = 1.6666667 and assumed capacity utilization 60%.
- Heat potential is 0.27 MW. No useful delivery, district-heating connection, heat tariff or demand is asserted.
- Ignore onsite electricity consumption, maintenance, ramping, CHP availability, fuel supply changes, electricity market fees and network charges; therefore revenue is gross and idealized.
- No gas buffer or dispatcher in v0.5. Fuel consumption is fixed at 0.6 MW times elapsed hours. Flaring and leakage are zero by modelling assumption, not observations.
- Historical provider observations are separate from model assumptions. Do not describe illustrative CHP parameters as Swedish plant measurements.
