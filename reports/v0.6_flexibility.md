# BiogasFlex v0.6 — Verification and research-boundary report

**Version:** 0.6.0, 5 November 2026. **Status:** local development candidate; no human peer review, no real-plant operational validation.

## Aim and frozen comparison rule

Quantify an **electricity-only perfect-foresight gross spot-equivalent revenue opportunity** under a gas buffer without changing gas supply, fixed losses/flaring, electrical conversion efficiency, nameplate rating, price series, or total inventory. Heat remains a **potential** co-product, not a proven served load. No CHP minimum load, ramp, wear/start costs, heat demand, onsite export accounting, gas vessel engineering or storage cost is included. All v0.6 models are continuous LPs; no commitment/start binaries.

## Units, equations and optimizer

Let time step `dt[t]` be in h, production `G[t]`, CHP utilization `U[t]`, flaring `F[t]` and loss `L[t]` in MW_LHV, gas inventory `S[t]` in MWh_LHV, electrical rating/output in MW_e, and market prices EUR/MWh_e:

- `S[t+1] = S[t] + (G[t] - U[t] - F[t] - L[t])*dt[t]`
- `0 <= U[t] <= Pmax/eta_e`, `0 <= S[t] <= Smax`, `S[0] = S[N] = S_initial`
- `P_e[t] = eta_e*U[t]`; potential `Q_th[t] = eta_th*U[t]`
- Objective `maximize sum(price[t] * eta_e*U[t] * dt[t])`.

Exogenous `F,L` are fixed and identical in optimized and fixed cases. The fixed comparator consumes `G-F-L` immediately and must fit within the generator rating. With cyclic storage, `sum(U*dt) = sum(G*dt)-sum(F*dt)-sum(L*dt)` (within tolerance), so any computed revenue difference comes exclusively from timing under model assumptions. If baseline cannot fit the rating, no uplift is published. CHP heat *potential* is recorded solely to make the co-production consequence visible; served heat is deferred to v0.7.

**Implementation:** SciPy 1.17.0 `linprog(method='highs')`. Roadmap suggested Pyomo/HiGHS; SciPy/HiGHS gives the same LP class with an independently auditable sparse constraint matrix but is a documented implementation change. No MILP claim.

### Prespecified numerical tolerances

- Maximum absolute per-interval gas conservation residual: `1e-8 MWh_LHV`.
- Absolute horizon gas balance and terminal inventory residual: `1e-8 MWh_LHV`.
- Maximum CHP nameplate exceedance: `1e-9 MW_e`.
- Solver objective versus independently reconstructed revenue: `1e-7 EUR`.
- Solver must explicitly return success and optimal status 0. Infeasibility and invalid baseline are **fatal**, never converted to a valid zero uplift.

## Mathematical oracle (all inputs synthetic)

Two one-hour intervals: gas input `G=[0.6, 0.6] MW_LHV`; electric efficiency `0.4`; nameplate `0.4 MW_e`; storage `0.4 MWh_LHV`; initial and terminal storage zero; prices `[50,200] EUR/MWh_e`.

Let `s=S1`. Conservation gives `U0=0.6-s`, `U1=0.6+s`, and nameplate gives `U1<=1.0` and `s<=0.4`. Revenue equals `50*0.4*(0.6-s) + 200*0.4*(0.6+s) = 60 + 60s`. Therefore `s=0.4` is the exact optimum, with CHP utilization `[0.2,1.0] MW_LHV` and stock `[0,0.4,0] MWh_LHV`. Baseline **EUR 60**, optimum **EUR 84**, uplift **EUR 24**, and identical gas consumed `1.2 MWh_LHV`.

Storage sweep `Smax=[0,0.1,0.2,0.4,0.8] MWh_LHV` gives uplift `[0,6,12,24,24] EUR`—an exact analytic upper envelope, with the expected plateau at nameplate headroom.

## Lövsta-labelled, source-mixed counterfactual (not real plant dispatch)

**Documented from published 2022 Lövsta information:** electrical nameplate 499 kW, annual electricity 3,002.6 MWh, annual heat 2,939.5 MWh. **Derived:** uniform 342.763 kW electrical output as annual mean, leaving nameplate-to-mean headroom. **Assumed:** gas production constant `0.856906 MW_LHV`, electrical efficiency 40%, heat coefficient fitted algebraically to the annual heat/electric ratio, storage `1 MWh_LHV`, no exogenous flaring/losses, ideal instant adjustable CHP fuel use and perfect foresight. **Observed independently in 2020:** one day of SE3 day-ahead prices under CC BY 4.0, 1 Jan 2020 local Swedish date, 24 one-hour periods, EUR/MWh_e.

These are **different years**. Nameplate minus annual mean is *not* proof of hourly flexibility; there is no evidence that Lövsta operated or could operate this schedule with the hypothetical buffer.

**Conditional gross electricity price-timing difference: EUR 2.0799803653 over 24 hours.** Gross spot-equivalent value is not actual plant electricity sales or net income. The assumed storage capacity is gas energy—not a dimensioned and certified pressure vessel. The hourly heat-output timing differs despite identical daily heat-potential energy; local thermal service feasibility is **not proven**.

Predeclared storage capacity sweep (MWh_LHV → EUR incremental gross value): `0 → 0`, `0.1 → 0.2164`, `0.25 → 0.5410`, `0.5 → 1.0781`, `1 → 2.0800`, `2 → 3.6901`. The 1 Jan 2020 price range was only EUR 24.81–30.46/MWh; this is a deliberately narrow observed-period example, not annual economic evidence. No annualization is justified.

### Risk and interpretation register

1. **Operational validation absent:** This is an illustrative CHP profile, not a calibrated time-series model of Lövsta.
2. **Heat-service omission:** Electricity-only optimum may be infeasible for useful heat delivery; v0.7 must handle this.
3. **Gas-vessel realism:** No measured capacity, pressure/volume, temperature, methane composition or engineering-standard sizing.
4. **Operating omissions:** CHP ramp/start, minimum load, parasitic loads, fuel availability, sales/export, fees and balancing ignored. 
5. **Market sampling:** One historical day, cross-year input pairing, perfect price foresight. Not a price forecast, Swedish regional policy result, profitability, resilience metric, LCOE or national flexibility estimate.
6. **Numerical correctness scope:** Automated and independent algebraic checks test the formulation, not whether physical assumptions describe a real facility.
