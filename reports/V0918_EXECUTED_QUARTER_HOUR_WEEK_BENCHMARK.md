# BiogasFlex v0.9.18 — Executed 24/48/168-hour synthetic optimizer audit

**Date:** 2026-11-15.
**Execution:** [full 96/192/672-slot week benchmark and independent
source-free witness, GitHub Actions run
43719038048](https://github.com/Open-Energy-Methods/biogasflex/actions/runs/43719038048).

The cases were generated by the versioned source program, solved
with the original unmodified SciPy/HiGHS heat-dispatch LP and
elapsed-hour MILP implementations, then evaluated by a **separate
standard-library script importing no solver or BiogasFlex module**.

This is **synthetic mathematical model verification**, not measured
plant flexibility or real electricity revenue.

All monetary values result from the explicitly fictional

nor an assessment of annual/site profitability.

The MILP gas pulses force 12 hours OFF and 12 hours ON in each
invented 24-hour period, with **zero buffer capacity**. Thus the
independent gas-conservation equations force 0/2 MW_LHV burn,
0/0.8 MW_e generation and exactly:

| Horizon | Forced ON starts | Forced OFF stops | Modeled fuel consumed (MWh_LHV) | Electricity (MWh_e) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 24 h | 1 | 0 | 24 | 9.6 |
| 48 h | 2 | 1 | 48 | 19.2 |
| 168 h | **7** | **6** | **168** | **67.2** |

The 168-hour exact closed-form net objective is:
`7×704 gross - 67.2 CHP variable - 37.8 auxiliary heat
- 21.0 dumped heat - 35 startups - 12 shutdowns = 4,755 EUR`.

The **gas flow and binary transitions** are uniquely fixed by
zero storage and pulsating production. Feasible heat-allocation
splits need not be unique, but the strictly positive auxiliary
and dumped heat penalties independently establish that **maximal
direct CHP heat service is cost optimal**. The algebraic witness
then certifies the objective globally for **this constrained
fictional case only**. This does *not* certify general MILP
branch-and-bound performance on nontrivial storage/commitment trade-offs.

## Independent numerical and negative-case audit

The first dedicated benchmark runner executed **9 models across
96, 192 and 672 intervals**, a combined **2,880 15-minute rows**.

The separate pure Python standard-library auditor verified:

- Per-quarter-hour gas energy conservation and cyclic inventory;
- Electrical `P_e=η_e·u`, thermal generation and
  contemporaneous heat-service constraints;
- Electric nameplate, gas-capacity, backup and heat-dump bounds;
- Steady-on quarter-hour midpoint ramp and on/off transitions;
- Exact startup/stop accounting and algebraic optimum of the
  zero-buffer commitment cases;
- Objective and all independently recomputed CHP/heat cost terms;
- Buffer-enabled LP primal feasibility and the validity of its
  reported unconstrained-LP upper bound.

Its reported status was
`PASS_INDEPENDENT_SYNTHETIC_MULTISCALE_RESEARCH_MODEL_WITNESSES`,
with **3 forced-case algebraic optimum witnesses** and **3
buffered-LP primal verifications**. The other 3 no-storage
LP models are also algebraically fixed by gas conservation,
although a distinct independent dual LP optimum is **not**
proven for the nonzero-storage cases.

All **3 intentionally impossible 96-slot models were rejected**:
first-OFF-hour insufficient backup heat, nameplate below forced
electrical production, and initial mandatory minimum-up event
requiring operation beyond the horizon.

The full-row benchmark JSON and distinct verification JSON are
attached to the GitHub Actions run above and are generated
outside the repository checkout. No raw plant data were used.
The derived electricity, gas and thermal quantities are not
source measurements.

## Disclosures and outstanding research

1. All case equipment parameters, price arrays, gas arrivals,
   heat demand and OPEX penalties are **invented** and do not
   come from the Japanese city-gas measured dataset, Polish
   landfill-gas CHP annual source, Matlink Farm or an identified
   Swedish biogas engine.
2. The currently modeled gas storage is an **energy inventory,
   not a pressure, vessel or safety dynamics model**.
3. Ramping applies only between continuously operating
   midpoints; transitions do **not** model startup physics.
4. This week-scale suite does **not** embed 672 real electricity
   awards; previous 2-slot hard-award contracts are retained as
   separate hypothetical, verified examples.
5. Nonzero-store LP runs are **HiGHS optimized and independently
   primal checked**, but no independent LP dual/KKT certificate
   has been delivered. Their objective should not be described
   as independently proven globally optimal.
6. Real gas-engine performance calibration, real operator
   measurements, measured site heating boundary and verified
   market settlement remain outstanding, so scientific v1.0
   **physical validation is not complete**.
