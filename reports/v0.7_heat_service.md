# BiogasFlex v0.7 — Heat-service and scenario-economics verification report

**Date:** 5 November 2026. **Stage:** local reproducible development candidate. **System boundary:** constant-gas-source CHP with gas buffer; synthetic contemporaneous thermal-service constraints; perfect-foresight continuous linear dispatch. Not validated plant operation, human peer review or financial investment advice.

## Research question

Does the v0.6 electricity-only upper-bound benefit survive when the same **hourly thermal service** must be supplied in both fixed and optimized scenarios, and any auxiliary heat or heat dumping is separately accounted for?

## New formulation

The legacy gas stock, electrical rating and cyclic inventory equations are unchanged. Introduce useful CHP heat `H_t`, backup `B_t`, rejected heat `J_t`, and heat service requirement `D_t`, all MW_th:

```text
H_t + J_t = eta_th * U_t
H_t + B_t = D_t
0 <= B_t <= Bcap_t, 0 <= J_t <= Jcap_t, 0 <= H_t <= D_t
```

Maximize `sum_t [spot_price_t * eta_e U_t - c_aux B_t - c_dump J_t - c_chp eta_e U_t] dt_t` with scenario-specified rates and coefficients. CHP heat and service balances are checked in the **independently recomputed ledger** rather than trusted from the solver matrix. The model allocates CHP heat to demand first to avoid artificial simultaneous backup and dumping when costs happen to be zero. Full model and error-contract: `docs/V07_MODEL_BOUNDARY.md`.

## Source-mixed 24-hour counterfactual (Lövsta parameters; *not* Lövsta operations)

Documented background: SLU 2024 thesis reports a 499 kWe nominal CHP rating and 2022 annual heat production 2,939.5 MWh and electricity 3,002.6 MWh. The model uses constant electric generation 342.76 kWe (annual mean), assumes 40% electrical efficiency and derives a constant gas input, fits the heat coefficient algebraically, and sets a **synthetic hourly heat-service requirement** equal in *magnitude* to the 2022 annual-average **produced** heat. It prices this 2022-derived hypothetical dispatch at **1 January 2020 SE3 observed market prices**. CHP backup cost EUR 80/MWh_th, 0.5 MW_th backup/sink capacity, 1 MWh_LHV buffer are **assumptions**.

| Quantity, modeled 24h | Fixed | Optimized heat-aware | Meaning |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Gross spot-equivalent electric value | EUR 228.1702 | EUR 228.1702 | Actual site sales unknown |
| Electricity generated | 8.2263 MWh_e | 8.2263 MWh_e | Constant-profile counterfactual |
| Heat-service requirement met | 8.0534 MWh_th | 8.0534 MWh_th | Synthetic demand proxy |
| Auxiliary heat supplied | approximately 0 | approximately 0 | Depends on assumed compatible heat/sink |
| **Incremental scenario contribution** | — | **EUR 0.00** | Numerical optimum (tolerance 1e-7 EUR) |

For comparison, the archived **electricity-only** v0.6 optimum under the same price window and gas assumptions was +**EUR 2.0800 gross**. The heat requirement and marginal backup economics exclude that shifting for this synthetic case. This is a valid **null scenario result**, not a claim that heat constraints universally remove CHP flexibility value, or that Lövsta actually faces this heat requirement/cost.

### Predeclared 24-case sensitivity

Grid: hourly heat demand factor `[0,0.75,1,1.1]` times the annual-average *heat production* proxy, and auxiliary heat variable cost `[0,5,10,20,80,200] EUR/MWh_th`. Recorded outcomes in `results/v0.7/lovsta_crossyear_heat_service_sensitivity.csv` and `results/v0.7/v07_study_summary.json`:

| Assumed hourly heat service factor | Backup cost EUR/MWh_th | Scenario contribution uplift |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0 (no heat obligation) | 80 | EUR 2.0800 |
| 0.75 | 80 | EUR 1.8197 |
| 1.00 | 5 | EUR 0.1466 |
| 1.00 | 20 | EUR 0.0000 |
| 1.00 | 80 | EUR 0.0000 |
| 1.10 | 80 | EUR 1.4807 |

The 1.10 case remains valuable despite expensive backup heat **because the optimized schedule shifts output without increasing *total backup MWh*** from the already heat-deficient comparator, within the assumed continuous LP. It does not establish an actual operating policy. At zero auxiliary marginal cost, backup use can rise substantially while the contribution objective remains indifferent; the reported gross electricity gain then omits real-world backup expenditures. Demand/cost choices are sensitivity parameters, not a fitted plant model. Do not choose or suppress cases to maximize an appealing outcome.

## Verification and audit result

- **81/81 automated unit/regression/integrity tests passed**, including 40 preserved v0.6/v0.5 tests. See `reports/v07_test_results.txt`.
- Separate standard-library script `scripts/independent_v07_audit.py` recomputes gas conservation, heat-service balance, electrical conversion, stock capacity, revenues and contribution independently from saved interval JSON. Output `reports/V07_INDEPENDENT_LEDGER_CHECK.json` confirms both 2h and 24h cases; targeted corruption tests demonstrate rejection of tampered heat values, revenues, gas stock and solver status.
- The thermal-service model independently rechecks solver objective against the measured-in-code ledger, raises on nonoptimal/infeasible comparators and validates explicit price interval boundaries, zone and units. Requires 1e-8 MWh gas/heat absolute residual and 1e-7 EUR objective recomputation.
- Regenerated CSV/JSON and figures do not require private plant data or market credentials, and retained inputs have recorded provenance and source attribution.
- **Review classification:** this is an AI-assisted, separate-code numerical audit; **no independent human scientist has reviewed the work**. Verification ≠ validation of observed heat-dispatch operations.

## Risks and omissions that block stronger claims

The model lacks site heat-use data, varying heat-recovery temperature, CHP part-load efficiency, minimum stable load, ramp times, starting fuel and wear costs, thermal buffering, physical gas volume/pressure and heat-sink constraints based on drawings, site-specific auxiliary-boiler fuels/tariffs, feedstock variation, methane leakage/emissions, plant electric on-site consumption vs export, balancing/deviation settlement, storage CAPEX and real price forecasts. It only evaluates one narrow historical day and an explicitly mismatched plant/reference year. No regional self-sufficiency, blackout resilience, GHG savings, full techno-economic feasibility or commercial profitability quantified. A numerical zero result is **conditional on assumptions** and cannot be generalized beyond them.

## Decision

Accept **v0.7 as local auditable development candidate** conditional on carrying these limitations forward. Do not attach to a PhD application as completed biological biogas research or describe it as externally peer reviewed. Next v0.8 should test predeclared price-window diversity, uncertain load and coefficients, and an explicitly defined energy-service shortage/disruption experiment. A larger research claim requires observed high-resolution gas/heat/electric data or clearly independent validation.
